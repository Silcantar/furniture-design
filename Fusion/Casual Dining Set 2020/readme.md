# Casual Dining (Breakfast Room) Set, 2020

This is the dining set I built for my kitchen/breakfast room. The trestle table borrows from several sources, although Nakashima may be the most important. The top is mesquite and the base is painted yellow pine.

Because I designed the set for a growing family, strength and stability were major considerations in the design. The wide stretchers and panels on the base allow it to be lightweight (both physically and visually) while maintaining excellent rigidity and stability. The extremely hard (2340 lbf Janka) mesquite top also lends excellent durability to the table.

The chairs are also made of strong, light, and cheap yellow pine. Because of the kicked-out back legs and mortise and tenon joinery they are much more stable than any store-bought chair I have sat in.
